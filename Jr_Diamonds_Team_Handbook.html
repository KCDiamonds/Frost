Part 1

Team Rules

Softball is the sport. These are the standards that make us a team worth being on.

Rule one

Respect the Game

  • Coaches Eye contact, and no interrupting
  • Each other Bullying = bye
  • Opponents Don't be nasty
  • Umpires Let the coach handle it
  • Your parents Their time, money, and support
Not on my field

Never Allowed

  • "I'm sorry" or "My bad"
  • Walking between the chalk
  • Throwing, slamming, or dropping equipment
  • Playing with malice
Rule two

Be an Example

Effort Attitude Body Language Emotional Regulation Time & Place Accountable, not sorry

Examples live here too: hustle, setup, equipment, and social media.

Not an example? Not on my field.

The only answer not allowed: "I don't know."

Be present, be focused, and get to the why.

More Standards

Clean upLeave every space better than you found it.

Part 2

Gameplay Strategy

Every game type has a job. Knowing the job explains the lineup.

Pool play
Strategic reps
  • Bat rotation in Game 1, adjust if needed
  • Rotate defense
Bracket play
Play to win
  • Bat 9 or 10 with DP/Flex
  • Play the top 3 strongest defenses
Scrimmages
Reps and R&D
  • Play 2nd to 3rd position
  • Working game style reps

How Playing Time Works

Coaches evaluate more than a statistic. Prepare for the answer if you ask.
No reps or positions are promised. They are earned in many ways.
Attitude and body language can and will get you pulled faster than mistakes.
Team first. This is a team sport. Strategy over feelings, and the best for the majority, while protecting and celebrating individual successes and achievements.
Guest players play. If we need guest players, they will play. They are not guest sitters.
